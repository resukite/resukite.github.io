Before you apply

An ATS resume checklist for freshers

Applicant tracking systems help employers organise applications. There is no single “ATS score,” but a simple, readable resume gives both software and people a better chance of understanding your experience.

Content checklist

  • Use a clear target role that matches the job you want.
  • Include current contact details and a professional email address.
  • Write a short, honest summary tailored to the role.
  • List your degree, college and expected graduation year.
  • Describe one or two projects with the tools used and a result.
  • Include only skills you can discuss in an interview.
  • Use relevant words from the job description naturally.

Formatting checklist

  • Keep your resume to one page when you are starting out.
  • Use simple headings such as Profile, Education, Skills and Projects.
  • Use readable text, consistent dates and standard bullet points.
  • Avoid charts, logos, text boxes and images that can make parsing harder.
  • Proofread names, links, phone number and spelling before exporting.
  • Save the final version as a PDF unless the employer asks for another format.

Final five-minute check

Read your resume once as a recruiter: can you see the role, degree, strongest skills and project proof in under 20 seconds? Then compare it with the job description one last time.
