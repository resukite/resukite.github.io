About ResuKite

Simple tools for first opportunities.

ResuKite is a free, browser-based resume builder made for students and freshers in India. It helps you create a clear one-page resume, preview it as you write, and save it as a PDF.

What we believe

Applying for a first job should not require paid software or complicated forms. We focus on straightforward guidance, clean formatting and practical examples. Our guides are educational; they do not guarantee interviews, job offers or an ATS result.

Your privacy

Your resume details are stored in your own browser so you can continue editing on the same device. Read our Privacy Policy for details.
