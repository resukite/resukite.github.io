Career guide

Resume summary examples for freshers

Use these only as inspiration. Keep your final summary accurate and specific to you.

A resume summary is a short introduction at the top of your resume. For a fresher, it should connect your education, relevant skills, a real project or achievement, and the role you want. Avoid claims such as “best” or “expert” unless you can prove them.

Software or IT fresher

Example: Computer science student with experience building responsive web projects using JavaScript, HTML and CSS. Completed a college event-discovery project and seeking a frontend internship to contribute to user-facing features.

Marketing fresher

Example: Business administration student interested in digital marketing, content writing and campaign analysis. Created social media posts for a college club and seeking an entry-level role where I can learn campaign execution and reporting.

Finance or accounts fresher

Example: Commerce graduate with coursework in accounting, Excel and financial reporting. Comfortable organising data and preparing basic reports; seeking a junior accounts or finance operations opportunity.

General graduate

Example: Recent graduate with strong communication, research and teamwork skills developed through college projects and volunteer work. Seeking an entry-level role where I can learn quickly and contribute reliably.

A simple formula

Education or current stage + relevant skills + proof + target role. Keep it to two or three lines. Then tailor the words to each job description without copying claims you cannot support.
