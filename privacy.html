Privacy Policy

ResumeKit India is a free resume-building tool. We collect as little data as possible.

Your resume information

The details entered in the builder are saved only in your browser using local storage. We do not receive, store, or sell your resume details.

Analytics and advertising

We do not currently run advertising or third-party analytics. If advertising is added in future, third-party advertising partners, including Google, may use cookies or similar technologies to show and measure ads. This policy will be updated before ads are enabled.
